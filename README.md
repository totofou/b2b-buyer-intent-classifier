# B2B Buyer Intent Classifier

Paste a list of SEO keywords → get each one tagged by **search intent**, and by
the thing that actually matters for B2B/export sites:
**does this keyword attract a buyer ready to source — or just a browser/consumer?**

Most intent tools stop at "informational / transactional."
For export & B2B, the real question is whether your keywords pull in **purchasers or window-shoppers.**

## Why this exists
I kept seeing B2B export sites with traffic but zero inquiries —
almost always because the keywords were attracting the wrong people from the start.
This makes that visible in seconds.

## What it does NOT do
It tells you *what's* wrong (your keywords may target the wrong crowd).
It does not decide *which* keywords to keep or cut for your specific site — that needs judgment on your traffic, goals, and competitors.
